Battery Pack Design & Thermal System

Purdue Electric Racing — reworking the pack architecture from cell layout through thermal validation.

RoleBattery Systems Lead, Purdue Electric Racing
TimeframeFall 2024 — Spring 2025
ToolsSimscape, SolidWorks, Arduino
01

Problem

The prior season's pack ran conservative cell spacing and an off-the-shelf thermal interface that left real energy density on the table and made the enclosure hard to seal cleanly for tech inspection.

02

Approach

Modeled pack behavior in Simscape before touching hardware, then redesigned the module layout to tighten cell spacing without compromising thermal paths. Paired the new layout with a custom thermal interface/spacer system, and validated cooling performance with an Arduino-instrumented test plate before committing to the final enclosure.

03

Result

30%
increase in pack energy density
10%
gain in cooling performance
100%
leak-free at tech inspection
