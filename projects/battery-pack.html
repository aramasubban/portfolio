Battery Pack Design & Thermal System

My time on Purdue Electric Racing’s battery team: fitting more energy into less space, keeping it cool, and getting it through tech inspection.

RoleBattery Senior Advisor
TimeframeAug 2023 — Dec 2025
FocusDesigning, building & testing battery modules
01

The challenge

A Formula SAE battery pack has to hold as much energy as possible, stay cool while the car is being pushed hard, and pass a strict tech inspection before it’s allowed on track. Every millimeter and every degree counts.

02

What I worked on

I started in simulation, building a Simscape model of the pack so we could see state of charge, heat generation and cell temperatures before building anything. From there I reworked the modules to take up less room: slimmer busbars sized from current and resistivity calculations, a tighter terminal busbar bend, and a staggered layout that made wire bonding easier.

Cooling came next. The cells can’t touch the cooling plate directly, so I designed a spacer and thermal interface system and picked the TIM thickness from a thermal conductivity analysis. I built an Arduino-based rig to check the cooling plate against edge cases, and designed the waterproof enclosure that sealed it all up.

03

The pack helped the team to a top-10 finish.
