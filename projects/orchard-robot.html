Orchard Laser Robot

A quadruped-mounted arm and perception pipeline for targeted laser treatment in orchards, built at Purdue MARS Lab.

We just switched the end effector from a sprayer to a laser. Photos and results go up here once it’s running.

01

Problem

Orchard treatment is usually blanket spraying: every plant gets chemical whether it needs it or not, which wastes material and increases exposure for everything nearby.

02

Approach

A robotic arm carried by a Unitree quadruped, paired with a perception pipeline built on DINO and SAM to detect and segment diseased foliage in the camera feed. The segmentation output becomes targets the arm controller can aim at.

The project started with a precision sprayer on the end of the arm; we've since shifted the scope to a laser end effector, which treats only the flagged regions and removes chemicals from the process entirely.

03

My part

I developed an automated gripper and cutter that holds the fruit while the stem is cut, driven by a custom servo linkage so the apple doesn’t drop.

I then integrated grasping, laser exposure and stem cutting into one coordinated operating sequence on the robot arm, and worked with a software teammate to trigger each cut from the SAM/DINO outputs running on the Unitree Go2 EDU.

04

Where it stands

Scope recently shifted from spraying to laser treatment. Integrating the laser end effector is the current milestone.
