Autonomous Orchard Laser Robot

Purdue MARS Lab — a quadruped-mounted arm and perception pipeline for targeted laser treatment in orchards. Work in progress.

RoleRobotics Researcher, Purdue MARS Lab
TimeframeSummer 2025 — Present
FocusPerception-guided robotic arm control
01

Problem

Orchard treatment is usually blanket spraying: every plant gets chemical whether it needs it or not, which wastes material and increases exposure for everything nearby.

02

Approach

A robotic arm carried by a Unitree quadruped, paired with a perception pipeline built on DINO and SAM to detect and segment diseased foliage in the camera feed. The segmentation output becomes targets the arm controller can aim at. The project started with a precision sprayer on the end of the arm; we've since shifted the scope to a laser end effector, which treats only the flagged regions and removes chemicals from the process entirely.

03

Result

In progress — scope recently shifted from spraying to laser treatment; integrating the laser end effector is the current milestone.
