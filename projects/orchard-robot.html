CS—02 / SPRAY

Autonomous Orchard Spraying Robot

Purdue MARS Lab — a sprayer end effector and perception pipeline that treats only diseased foliage.

RoleRobotics Researcher, Purdue MARS Lab
TimeframeSummer 2025 — Present
ToolsROS2, DINO / SAM, Unitree quadruped
01

Problem

Orchard spraying is usually blanket coverage — every plant gets treated whether it needs it or not, which wastes chemical and increases exposure for anything nearby.

02

Approach

Mounted a sprayer end effector on a robotic arm carried by a Unitree quadruped, then built a perception pipeline on top of DINO and SAM to detect and segment diseased foliage in the camera feed. The segmentation output is converted into targets the arm controller can aim at, so the sprayer only fires on regions flagged as infected.

03

Result

In progress — perception pipeline validated on collected orchard imagery; field integration with the arm controller is the current milestone.
