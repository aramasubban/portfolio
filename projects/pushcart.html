The Pushcart Project

A self-directed build at 16: an electric waste-cart for under $2,000, designed for the older workers at a local market in Bangalore and donated into daily use.

Role
Founder / Sole Designer
When
2021, age 16
Budget
$2,000 from the local government
Where
Bangalore, India
Focus
An e-cart built for real people, on a real budget
01

Problem

Waste collectors in Bangalore push loaded ICE carts through dense neighborhoods for hours a day — expensive to run, unpleasant to operate, and hard on the people pushing them.

02

Approach

Designed a functional electric replacement from scratch. I analyzed the road surfaces the cart would actually run on to size the motor and set the battery range requirement, then designed a custom steering rack and the overall layout.

I iterated the frame and drivetrain in CAD with manufacturability in mind, sourced a motor and battery pack that kept the total build under the $2,000 budget, and built and tested the cart myself before donating it into daily use.

Result
$1,850total build cost
1cart deployed & in daily use
0tailpipe emissions vs. ICE cart
