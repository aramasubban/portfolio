The Pushcart Project

A self-directed build: an electric waste-cart for under $2,000, designed and deployed in Bangalore.

RoleFounder / Sole Designer
TimeframeSummer 2023
ToolsSolidWorks, DFM, field testing
01

Problem

Waste collectors in Bangalore push loaded ICE carts through dense neighborhoods for hours a day — expensive to run, unpleasant to operate, and hard on the people pushing them.

02

Approach

Designed a functional electric replacement from scratch: sketched the layout, iterated the frame and drivetrain in CAD with manufacturability in mind, sourced a motor and battery pack that kept the total build under $2,000, and built and tested the cart myself before donating it into daily use.

03

Result
