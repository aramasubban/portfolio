What I worked on

6 projects
01
Yield

Fixing yield loss in laser welding

Cell laser welding was losing a lot of yield, and nobody had a clean answer for why. I owned the root-cause analysis, ran DOEs, and dug through the data in JMP until the pattern showed up: the exhaust flow. I optimized it, validated weld quality with pull tests and dust checks, and pushed the change to production. Downstream yield went up by about 10,000 good cells a week.

02
Automation

A jig that fixes misaligned current collectors

A supplier process was sending us current collectors that weren’t always lined up, and that one issue was the single biggest availability and scrap loss on the floor. Instead of waiting on the supplier, I engineered a production jig with timed vibration control that centers four tubes in 30 seconds. In production trials it ran 60,000 cells with zero pickup faults.

03
Quality

A conveyor-cleaning tool to cut cell contamination

Grease on a conveyor was getting into cells. I designed and deployed a cleaning tool that rides on the shuttle and wipes the conveyor with non-shedding foam as the line runs. Maintenance downtime dropped 25%, and the monthly cleaning job went from two hours to 15 minutes. It was one piece of the push to raise cell output for Cybercab.

04
Inspection

Proving a fastener upgrade with an accelerated test

A fastener on the inspection equipment kept loosening from vibration, which made the station reject good cells. Waiting for it to fail on its own wasn’t an option, so I built an IMU-based accelerated test that packs six months of runtime into a short run, then used it to qualify a fleetwide fastener upgrade. Inspection yield rose by about 10%.

05
Reliability

Protecting the winders

A recurring failure was damaging shuttles at the winders, and it showed up across several equipment generations. I redesigned the winder protection and standardized a compression-fit tray through rapid prototyping and production trials. Damage dropped 20%, and a damaged tray can now be replaced the same day.

06
Weld quality

Standardizing laser weld power across the fleet

Laser welders across the fleet weren’t all running at the same power, and it showed up as uneven weld strength. I led the effort to standardize weld power and recalibrated each tool myself, which made the whole fleet noticeably more consistent.
